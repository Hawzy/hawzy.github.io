instructions:
  - "In a small bowl, whisk together the zest and juice from the fresh limes `2 limes`, raw wildflower honey `2 tbsp`, finely minced garlic `3 cloves`, and crushed red chile pepper flakes `1/2 tsp` until the honey dissolves into a glossy amber glaze."
  - "Pat the skin-on Atlantic salmon fillets `4 fillets (6 oz each)` bone-dry on both sides with paper towels until the skin feels matte, then season the flesh evenly with coarse kosher salt `1 tsp`."
  - "Heat extra virgin olive oil `1 tbsp` in a large stainless-steel or cast-iron skillet over **medium-high heat** for **2 minutes** until shimmering."
  - "Lay the seasoned salmon fillets skin-side down into the hot skillet, pressing gently on each fillet with a flexible fish spatula for **10–15 seconds** so the skin stays flat against the metal without curling."
  - "Sear undisturbed over **medium-high heat** for **5–6 minutes** until the skin turns shatteringly crisp and deep golden-brown at the edges and an opaque pale-pink cook line rises three-quarters up the sides."
  - "Flip the fillets flesh-side down and immediately pour the whisked citrus-chile glaze into the skillet around (not over) the crisp skin."
  - "Simmer rapidly for **2–3 minutes**, spooning the bubbling garlic-lime glaze around the fish until reduced to a sticky, syrupy lacquer that coats the back of a spoon and the thickest center registers **125°F** on an instant-read thermometer."
  - "Transfer the salmon skin-side up onto warmed plates to preserve the crackly crust, and spoon the glossy citrus-chile pan lacquer around the fillets immediately before serving."

Crispy-skin salmon paired with a tangy, spicy, and sticky glaze is the quintessential weeknight dinner. The key to shatteringly crisp skin is two-fold:

1. **Moisture Removal:** Ensure the skin is meticulously dried with paper towels before hitting the hot skillet. Any residual surface moisture creates steam, which softens the skin rather than frying it crisp.
2. **Contact Pressure:** When salmon fillets meet hot oil, the skin contracts instantly, causing the fish to curl upwards. Gently pressing down with a flexible fish spatula during the first 10-15 seconds keeps the entire skin surface in continuous contact with the pan.

### The Citrus-Chile Glaze
By reducing raw wildflower honey directly with fresh lime juice, grated zest, fresh garlic, and crushed chile flakes in the final two minutes of pan-frying, the sugars caramelize without scorching, creating a lacquer-like glaze that clings perfectly to every forkful.
