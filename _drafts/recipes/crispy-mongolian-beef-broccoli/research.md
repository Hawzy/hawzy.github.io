# Culinary Research Dossier — Crispy Mongolian Beef with Broccoli and White Onion

- **Yield:** `4 servings` `[s1, s3]`
- **Prep / Cook / Total Time:** `20 minutes` active prep (plus `20 minutes` freezer firming and `30 minutes` chill) / `15 minutes` cook / `35 minutes` active total `[s1, s3, s4]`

---

## 1. Protein Fabrication & Dual-Stage Velveting (`1 lb` Flank Steak)

- **Cut & Cryogenic Firming**: `1 lb` (`450 g`) trimmed beef flank steak (`Musculus rectus abdominis`) is placed in the freezer for `20 minutes` (`15 to 30 minutes` range) to induce partial sarcoplasmic ice crystallization for clean slicing `[s1]`. If the steak is wider than `3 inches`, halve it lengthwise *with* the grain first `[s5]`, then slice strictly across the grain on a `45-degree` bias into `1/4-inch` (`6 mm`) strips `[s1, s5]`.
- **Stage 1 — Wet Alkaline Velveting Marinade**:
  - `1/4 tsp` pure baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) per `1 lb` beef raises surface pH to prevent actin/myosin filament contraction without imparting a soapy off-flavor `[s1, s6]`.
  - Hydrating and aromatic marinade: `2 tsp` dry Marsala wine (or dry sherry / Shaoxing rice wine) `[s1, s5]`, `1 tbsp` low-sodium soy sauce `[s1]`, `1 tbsp` neutral oil `[s1]`, `1 tbsp` cornstarch `[s1]`, and `1 tbsp` cold water `[s1]`.
  - Massage vigorously by hand for `1 minute` (`60 seconds`) until absorbed into a tacky sheen, then cover and refrigerate for `30 minutes` `[s1, s6]`.
- **Stage 2 — Secondary Dry Cornstarch Dredge**:
  - Immediately prior to frying, if the marinated beef surface feels dry rather than tacky, work in `1 to 2 tsp` water `[s2]`, then toss in `6 tbsp` (`3/8 cup`, out of `1/2 cup` total divided cornstarch) dry cornstarch until every strip is coated in a chalky-white layer `[s1, s4, s6]`.

---

## 2. Mongolian Glaze & Thickening Slurry

- **Balanced Sugar-to-Liquid Ratio**: Commercial takeout formulas often use a cloying 1:1 soy-to-sugar ratio (`1/2 cup` each) `[s3]`. This formulation uses `1/4 cup` (`50 g`) packed light brown sugar dissolved into `1/3 cup` warm low-sodium chicken bone broth `[s1, s4]`, paired with `1/4 cup` (`4 tbsp`) low-sodium soy sauce `[s1]`, `1 1/2 tsp` unsulfured molasses (or blackstrap molasses / Chinese dark soy sauce for mahogany color without excess sodium) `[s1, s5]`, and `1 tsp` pure toasted sesame oil `[s1, s5]`.
- **Cornstarch Slurry**: `1 tbsp` cornstarch stirred with `1 tbsp` cold water in a separate cup, streamed into the simmering glaze for `30 to 45 seconds` until glossy and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon `[s1, s2]`.

## 3. Vegetable Moisture Control & Thermal Wok Sequencing

- **Broccoli Steam-Blistering (`1 lb` / `4 cups` florets)**:
  - Heat a `12-inch` heavy cast-iron skillet or carbon-steel wok over **high heat** until smoking `[s3, s5]`. Swirl in `1 tbsp` neutral oil, add `1 lb` (`4 cups`) `1-inch` broccoli florets, and stir-fry briskly for `60 seconds` until spot-charred `[s3]`.
  - Splash `2 tbsp` cold water down the pan wall, cover with a lid, and steam for `90 seconds` (`60 to 90 seconds`) until bright emerald and crisp-tender; evacuate to a plate and wipe the pan dry `[s3]`.
- **Two-Batch Protein Shallow-Fry**:
  - Heat `6 tbsp` neutral oil (`1/2 cup` total divided oil: `1 tbsp` marinade + `1 tbsp` broccoli + `6 tbsp` shallow-fry) over **high heat** until shimmering with wisps of white smoke `[s1, s4]`.
  - Fry beef in 2 uncrowded batches for `2 minutes` undisturbed on the first side until a golden-brown craggy crust forms, then flip and sear `60 seconds` on the second side `[s1, s4]`. Thin `1/4-inch` strips quickly exceed the `145°F` USDA minimum internal temperature for beef `[s7]` while staying tender inside their starch crust.
  - Drain on a tilted rimmed baking sheet (not paper towels, which trap steam and soften the bottom crust) `[s2]`.
- **Sequential Allium Blooming & Final Emulsification**:
  - Pour off frying oil, retaining `1 tbsp` seasoned oil in the pan over **medium-high heat** `[s1]`.
  - Stir-fry `1 medium` (`5 oz`) white onion sliced into `1/4-inch` petals for `60 seconds` until crisp-tender and translucent at the edges `[s2]`.
  - Push onions to the perimeter; bloom `1 tbsp` finely minced fresh ginger and `4 whole` dried red chili peppers in the center oil pool for `15 seconds` `[s1]`.
  - Add `4 cloves` finely chopped garlic and `6` scallion whites (`1-inch` bias cuts) and toss for `30 seconds` `[s1]`.
  - Pour in the Mongolian glaze and simmer over **medium-high heat** for `60 seconds`, then stream in the re-stirred cornstarch slurry and cook `30 to 45 seconds` until thickened into a dark mahogany lacquer `[s1, s2]`.
  - Increase to **high heat**, return the crispy beef, blistered broccoli, and `6` scallion greens (`1 1/2-inch` bias cuts), and toss vigorously for `30 to 45 seconds` until every craggy ridge locks onto the glaze with zero standing liquid `[s1, s4]`.
