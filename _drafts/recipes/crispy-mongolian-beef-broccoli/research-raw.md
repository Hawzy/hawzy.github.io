# Technical Report Handoff — Crispy Mongolian Beef with Flank Steak, Alliums, Broccoli, and Steamed White Rice

Extracted from user-provided PDF: *Culinary Engineering and Gastronomic Analysis: Master Formulation for Mongolian Beef with Flank Steak, Alliums, Broccoli, and Steamed White Rice* (18 pages, 41 cited sources).

## Key Formulation Parameters from Report
- **Protein & Fabrication**: 1 lb (450 g) beef flank steak, frozen 15–30 minutes (20 minutes optimal) for cryogenic firming, bisected lengthwise with the grain if wider than 3 inches, sliced 1/4-inch (6 mm) thick perpendicular to the grain on a 45-degree bias `[1, 6, 13, 19]`.
- **Dual-Stage Velveting & Crust**:
  - Stage 1 (Alkaline Marinade, 30–60 min refrigerated): 1/4 tsp baking soda per lb beef, 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry / dry Marsala), 1 tsp–1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp water, 1 tbsp cornstarch, 2 tsp–1 tbsp neutral oil, massaged 60 seconds `[6, 16, 25]`.
  - Stage 2 (Dry Cornstarch Dredge): 1/3 to 1/2 cup (6–8 tbsp / 60 g) dry cornstarch immediately before shallow-frying; if marinated beef surface is dry, work in 1–2 tsp water first `[6, 7]`.
- **Mongolian Glaze & Slurry**:
  - Balanced 1:4 sugar-to-liquid ratio instead of cloying 1:1 takeout ratios `[6, 9]`: 1/4 cup (50 g) packed light brown sugar dissolved in warm/hot low-sodium chicken stock or water (1/3 to 3/4 cup), 1/4 cup (60 ml) low-sodium soy sauce, 1.5 tsp dark soy sauce or unsulfured/blackstrap molasses `[6, 19]`, and 1 tsp toasted sesame oil `[6, 19]`.
  - Slurry: 1 tbsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp cold water `[6, 26]`.
- **Cruciferous & Allium Partitioning**:
  - 12–16 oz (1 lb, ~4 cups) fresh broccoli crowns cut into 1 to 1.5-inch bite-sized florets; blistered in 1 tbsp oil over high heat for 60 seconds, splashed with 2 tbsp water and covered for 60–90 seconds until bright emerald and crisp-tender, then evacuated `[9, 26]`.
  - Shallow-fry beef in 6–8 tbsp (1/3–1/2 cup) smoking neutral oil in 2 uncrowded batches (60–120s first side, 30–60s second side) and drain on a tilted rimmed baking sheet (not paper towels) `[6, 7]`.
  - Retain 1 to 1.5 tbsp oil; stir-fry 1 medium white onion (sliced into 1/4–1/2-inch petals) for 60 seconds `[8, 12]`; bloom 1 tbsp minced fresh ginger and 4–6 whole dried red chilies for 15 seconds `[6]`; add 4 cloves chopped garlic and 6 scallion whites (1-inch bias pieces) for 10–30 seconds `[6]`.
  - Simmer glaze 60–120 seconds, stream in re-stirred cornstarch slurry for 15–45 seconds until glossy, then toss beef, broccoli, and 6 scallion greens (1.5-inch bias pieces) over high heat for 30–45 seconds `[6, 7]`.
