# Technical Report Handoff — Executing Shabu-Shabu Steak and Egg Fried Rice on a Flat-Top Griddle

Extracted from user-provided PDF: *Technical Culinary Report: Executing Shabu-Shabu Steak and Egg Fried Rice on a Flat-Top Griddle* (15 pages, 48 cited sources).

## Key Formulation Parameters from Report
- **Costco Octopus Sauce (Bachan's Original Japanese Barbecue Sauce, Costco #1566350)**:
  - Cold-filled without cornstarch or xanthan gum thickeners `[4, 10, 13]`; contains 7–8 g sugar (14–17 g per 2 tbsp) and 520 mg sodium per 1 tbsp (19 g) `[4, 11, 14, 16]`, 0 g fat `[11]`.
  - Scorches and turns bitter if applied early on 400°F+ steel `[2, 4]`. Must be used strictly as a late-stage finishing glaze (`2.5 tbsp` divided: `1 tbsp` on steak for final 15 seconds, `1.5 tbsp` on rice for final 60–90 seconds) `[2, 3, 9]`.
- **Costco Shabu-Shabu Thin-Sliced Beef (`1 lb` / `450 g`)**:
  - Fresh butcher-counter ribeye, NY strip, or top round sliced ~`1/8 inch` (`3 mm`) thick `[5, 9]`.
  - Separate ribbons before griddling, pat dry, season lightly with `1/2 tsp` kosher salt, `1/2 tsp` coarse black pepper, and `1/2 tsp` garlic powder (or SPG) `[5, 9, 24]`.
  - Flash-sear in a single uncrowded layer over smoking-hot steel (`425°F–450°F+`) in `1 tbsp` neutral oil for `30 to 45 seconds` undisturbed, flip and chop on steel with two spatulas (`60 to 90 seconds` total to ~80% doneness), glaze `15 seconds` with `1 tbsp` low-sodium soy sauce + `1 tbsp` Bachan's, then park on low-heat zone `[1, 2, 6, 9]`.
- **Two-Zone Thermal Configuration (`10 minutes` preheat)**:
  - Left burners (Burners 1 & 2): **Medium-High to High** (`425°F–450°F`, lowered to `375°F–400°F` for rice toasting) `[1, 3]`.
  - Right burners (Burners 3 & 4): **Low** (`275°F–300°F`) for holding sautéed vegetables and seared steak `[1]`.
- **Hibachi Garlic Butter & Seasoning Matrix**:
  - Compound garlic butter: `4 tbsp` (`1/2 stick`) softened unsalted butter mashed with `4 cloves` finely grated garlic and `1 tsp` fresh lemon juice `[1, 3, 6, 7]` (divided: `1/2 tbsp` for vegetables, `2 tbsp` for rice toasting, `1 1/2 tbsp` for egg scramble).
  - Starch-retrograded cold cooked rice: `5 cups` (`4 to 5 cups` chilled 12–24 hours, clumps broken up by hand) `[1, 2, 18]`, coated in `2 tbsp` garlic butter + optional `1 1/2 tbsp` Kewpie Japanese mayonnaise, pressed flat and fried undisturbed `3 to 4 minutes` for crispy *okoge* crust `[1, 18, 35]`.
  - Vegetables: `1 medium` diced yellow onion (`1/4-inch`) and `6` scallion whites sautéed in `1 tbsp` neutral oil for `2 to 3 minutes`; `1 1/2 cups` frozen peas and carrots added straight from freezer with `1/2 tbsp` garlic butter and tossed `90 to 120 seconds` until ice crystals evaporate and peas turn bright green `[1, 3, 6, 7, 18]`.
  - Eggs: `4 large` eggs beaten until uniform, poured into a `6-inch` center well with `1 1/2 tbsp` foaming garlic butter, set `20 to 30 seconds`, scrambled into soft glossy curds (~85% set), chopped and folded into rice `[3, 6, 9]`.
  - Final Amalgamation & Off-Heat Aromatics: Combine steak and vegetables on rice; season with `1 tsp` Knorr chicken bouillon powder, `1/2 tsp` ground white pepper, `1 1/2 tbsp` low-sodium soy sauce, and `1 1/2 tbsp` Bachan's sauce; toss over **high heat** for `60 to 90 seconds` `[2, 3, 9, 29, 33]`. Turn off all burners and finish off-heat with `2 tsp` pure toasted sesame oil, `2 tsp` fresh lemon juice (`1/2 lemon` total divided: `1 tsp` in butter + `2 tsp` off-heat finish), `6` sliced scallion greens, and `1 tbsp` toasted sesame seeds `[3, 18]`.
