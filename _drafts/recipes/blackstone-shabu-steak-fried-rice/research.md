# Culinary Research Dossier — Blackstone Shabu Steak and Egg Fried Rice

- **Slug:** `blackstone-shabu-steak-fried-rice`
- **Origin:** `research` (Imported from 15-page Technical Culinary Report PDF)
- **Yield:** `4 servings` `[s1]`
- **Prep / Cook / Total Time:** `15 minutes` prep / `20 minutes` cook (`10 minutes` griddle preheat + `10 minutes` active griddling) / `35 minutes` total `[s1, s2, s3]`

---

## 1. Bachan's Japanese Barbecue Sauce Chemistry & Glaze Timing

- **Formulation & Sugar/Sodium Density**: Bachan's Original Japanese Barbecue Sauce (Costco 34 oz "octopus" bottle) is cold-filled without starch thickeners (`35 to 40 kcal`, `7 to 8 g` sugar, `520 mg` sodium, and `0 g` fat per `1 tbsp` / `19 g` serving) `[s4]`.
- **Pyrolysis Prevention**: Pouring Bachan's onto `400°F+` steel early causes its cane sugar, mirin, and tomato paste solids to scorch and turn bitter before rice crisps `[s3, s4]`.
- **Divided Application (`2 1/2 tbsp` total)**:
  - `1 tbsp` Bachan's + `1 tbsp` low-sodium soy sauce glazed onto searing shabu-shabu beef for the final `15 seconds` `[s1, s4]`.
  - `1 1/2 tbsp` Bachan's + `1 1/2 tbsp` low-sodium soy sauce poured around the hot steel perimeter and over the rice during the final `60 to 90 seconds` high-heat toss `[s1, s3, s4]`.

---

## 2. Hibachi Garlic Butter, Seasoning Bridge & Counterweights

- **Part 1 — Hibachi Garlic Butter**: Mash `4 tbsp` (`1/2 stick`) softened unsalted butter with `4 cloves` finely grated garlic and `1 tsp` fresh lemon juice (from `1/2` fresh lemon yielding `1 tbsp` / `3 tsp` total juice, divided) `[s2, s3]`.
  - Divided across 3 griddle stages: `1/2 tbsp` for frozen peas and carrots, `2 tbsp` for toasting cold rice, and `1 1/2 tbsp` for scrambling eggs `[s1, s2, s3]`.
- **Umami & Spice Bridge**: `1 tsp` Knorr chicken bouillon powder (or a pinch of MSG) + `1/2 tsp` ground white pepper sprinkled evenly across the rice before the final glaze toss `[s7]`.
- **Kewpie Mayonnaise & Off-Heat Aromatics**:
  - `1 1/2 tbsp` Kewpie Japanese mayonnaise (optional) folded into the warming rice alongside `2 tbsp` garlic butter to coat grains in egg-yolk lipids and accelerate Maillard crusting `[s6]`.
  - Strictly **off-heat** after shutting off all burners: drizzle `2 tsp` pure toasted sesame oil and the remaining `2 tsp` fresh lemon juice, and scatter `6` sliced scallion greens and `1 tbsp` toasted sesame seeds `[s3, s5]`.

---

## 3. Two-Zone Flat-Top Protocol, Protein Sear & Starch Retrogradation

- **Griddle Thermal Zoning (`10 minutes` preheat)**:
  - Left burners: **medium-high to high** (`425°F to 450°F`) until lightly smoking `[s1]`.
  - Right burners: **low** (`275°F to 300°F`) as a holding zone `[s1]`.
- **Vegetable Dehydration (`1 tbsp` neutral oil + `1/2 tbsp` garlic butter)**:
  - Sauté `1 medium` (`1 cup`) `1/4-inch` diced yellow onion and `6` sliced scallion whites in `1 tbsp` neutral oil over the hot zone for `2 to 3 minutes` until translucent and charred at the edges `[s2, s5]`.
  - Add `1 1/2 cups` frozen peas and carrots straight from the freezer (do not thaw in water) with `1/2 tbsp` garlic butter and toss `90 to 120 seconds` until ice crystals cook off and peas turn bright green; slide to the low-heat side `[s1, s2]`.
- **Costco Shabu-Shabu Steak Flash-Sear (`1 lb` / `450 g`)**:
  - Pull `1 lb` (`1/8-inch` / `3 mm` thick) shabu-shabu beef (ribeye, NY strip, or top round) into loose single ribbons, pat dry, and season with `1/2 tsp` Diamond Crystal kosher salt, `1/2 tsp` coarse black pepper, and `1/2 tsp` garlic powder `[s1]`.
  - Over **high** heat in the remaining `1 tbsp` neutral oil, sear in a single uncrowded layer undisturbed for `30 to 45 seconds` until deeply browned, then flip and chop with two spatulas (`60 to 90 seconds` total to ~80% doneness) `[s1]`. Glaze for `15 seconds` with `1 tbsp` low-sodium soy sauce and `1 tbsp` Bachan's, then park on top of the vegetables on the low side `[s1, s4]`. Thin `1/8-inch` ribbons seared on `450°F` steel and finished in the `60 to 90 second` high-heat rice toss reach the `145°F` USDA beef safety baseline `[s8]` without turning gray or rubbery.
- **Rice Toasting & Egg Well (`5 cups` cold rice + `4 large` eggs)**:
  - Lower left burners to **medium-high** (`375°F to 400°F`), melt `2 tbsp` garlic butter, add `5 cups` cold day-old cooked rice (clumps broken up by hand), fold in `1 1/2 tbsp` Kewpie mayo if using, press flat with spatulas, and fry undisturbed `3 to 4 minutes` until crisp golden *okoge* bits form underneath `[s1, s3, s5, s6]`.
  - Push rice outward to form a `6-inch` center well, melt the remaining `1 1/2 tbsp` garlic butter, pour in `4 large` beaten eggs, let set `20 to 30 seconds`, gently scramble to ~85% set soft curds, chop, and fold rice over them `[s1, s3]` (finishing past `160°F` USDA egg safety baseline `[s8]` during the final high-heat toss).

## Disagreements

| Topic | Source A | Source B | Chosen Resolution & Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bachan's volume (`4+ tbsp` sole sauce vs. `2 1/2 tbsp` split with soy & bouillon)** | `4+ tbsp` Bachan's as sole seasoning (`Reddit r/Costco threads`) | `2 1/2 tbsp` Bachan's paired with `2 1/2 tbsp` low-sodium soy sauce + `1 tsp` Knorr bouillon (`Blackstone [s1]`, `Matt Hussey [s7]`) | **`2 1/2 tbsp` Bachan's + `2 1/2 tbsp` low-sodium soy + `1 tsp` chicken bouillon `[s1, s4, s7]`** — cuts free sugar density in half (preventing scorching on `400°F+` steel) while preserving deep savory teppanyaki salinity. |
| **Soy sauce in compound garlic butter vs. griddle pours** | `1/2 tsp` soy mashed into butter (`Johnny Brunet [s3]`) | Pure unsalted butter + garlic + lemon in bowl; all `2 1/2 tbsp` soy poured directly on steel (`The Flat Top King [s2]`) | **Keep all `2 1/2 tbsp` low-sodium soy for the griddle (`1 tbsp` on steak + `1 1/2 tbsp` on rice) `[s1, s2]`** — avoids a 3-way split of soy sauce and lets the unsalted butter brown cleanly without early soy scorching. |
| **Thawing vs. keeping peas & carrots frozen** | Thawed peas and carrots (`Johnny Brunet [s3]`) | Straight from freezer onto dedicated griddle zone (`The Flat Top King [s2]`) | **Straight from freezer (`1 1/2 cups`) `[s2]`** — thawing in water waterlogs the vegetables; flashing frozen peas and carrots on bare steel for `90 to 120 seconds` evaporates surface ice while keeping peas snappy. |
