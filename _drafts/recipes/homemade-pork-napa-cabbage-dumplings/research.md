# Culinary Research Dossier — Homemade Pork and Napa Cabbage Dumplings (Potstickers & Steamed)

- **Slug:** `homemade-pork-napa-cabbage-dumplings`
- **Origin:** `research` (Imported from 26-page Technical Culinary Blueprint PDF)
- **Yield:** `36 dumplings` (`6 servings` of `6 dumplings`, or `4 hearty main servings` of `9 dumplings`) `[s1, s3, s4]`
- **Prep / Cook / Total Time:** `120 minutes` solo prep (including `~15m` active aromatic/cabbage knife mise en place, `5m` dough scald/temper + `25m` autolyse overlapping concurrent `15m` scallion-ginger steep and `15m` cabbage salt-purge, `4m` knead + `30m` dough relaxation overlapping `8m` pork myosin/`da shui` whip, `2m` seasoning/oil seal, and `20m` chill, `3m` fold & `15–20s` microwave test, and `~42m` solo rolling & pleating `4` quarters of `9` dumplings) / `30 minutes` cook (`3` skillet batches of `12` potstickers × `~9.5 to 10 minutes` per batch; single pan batch `~10 minutes`) / `150 minutes` total `[s2, s3, s4, s5]`

## 1. Hybrid Half-Scalded Wrapper Dough (`Bantangmian`, 58.3% Hydration)

- **Exact Formulation (`36` Wrappers, `478 g` Total Dough)**:
  - `300 g` (`~2 1/2 cups` spooned and leveled) all-purpose flour (`10% to 13.3%` US protein) + `3 g` (`1/2 tsp`) fine sea salt whisked in a wide bowl set on a damp towel `[s3, s4, s6]`.
  - **Scald Center**: Pour `120 g` (`120 mL` / `1/2 cup`) freshly boiling water (`212°F` / `100°C`, weighed immediately after boiling) in a steady stream into the center of the flour while swirling with chopsticks, leaving dry flour around the perimeter `[s3, s4]`. Scalding denatures gluten proteins and pre-gelatinizes wheat starch (`124°F to 194°F`) to eliminate elastic snap-back `[s2, s3]`.
  - **Temper Perimeter**: Immediately drizzle `55 g` (`55 mL` / `3 1/2 tbsp`, plus `1 to 2 tsp` if dry flour remains) cool/room-temperature water around the outer edge and swirl into shaggy flakes so partial gluten survives for tensile strength `[s3, s4]`.
- **Two-Stage Rest & Tapered Rolling**:
  - Press into a rough ball, cover, and rest `20 to 30 minutes` (autolyse) `[s4]`.
  - Knead on a clean board `3 to 5 minutes` until smooth with "earlobe softness"; divide into `4` equal quarters (`~119 g` each), wrap tightly, and rest `30 to 60 minutes` at room temperature (or up to `24 hours` chilled, brought back to room temp before rolling) `[s3, s4]`. Do **not** re-knead after the final rest `[s6]`.
  - Work `1` quarter (`9` wrappers) at a time while keeping the rest sealed: roll into a `1-inch` (`2.5 cm`) rope, cut into `9` medallions (`3/4-inch` / `2 cm`, `~13 g` each) rotating the rope `90 degrees` after every cut, palm-flatten cut-side up to `1.5 inches` (`4 cm`), roll to `2.5 inches` (`6 cm`), then roll-and-rotate only the outer perimeter to `4 inches` (`10 cm`, within `3.5 to 4.5 inches` / `9 to 11 cm`) with a `2 mm` (`1/16- to 1/8-inch`) center belly and `1 to 1.5 mm` thin edges `[s2, s3, s4, s6]`.
  - Fill and pleat all `9` wrappers from each quarter under a damp towel before unwrapping the next quarter; if stacking ahead, dust exclusively with cornstarch or potato starch, never wheat flour `[s3, s6]`.

## 2. Four-Layer Northern Pork & Napa Cabbage Filling (`~914 g` Net Yield)

- **Aromatic Scallion-Ginger Water (`75 mL` / `5 tbsp` Strained & Chilled)**:
  - Steep `2` bruised scallions, `15 g` (`1 tbsp` / `1/2-inch` knob) smashed ginger, and `1 tsp` Sichuan peppercorns (optional) in `100 mL` (`scant 1/2 cup`) hot water for `15 minutes` until fragrant, muddling briefly; strain `75 mL` (`5 tbsp`) and chill in the refrigerator until cold before whipping into cold pork `[s1, s9]`.
- **Osmotic Cabbage Purge (`450 g` Raw -> `~230 to 250 g` Squeezed)**:
  - Reserve `6 to 8` whole outer napa cabbage leaves from the remaining half head if lining a bamboo steamer `[s7]`. Finely mince `450 g` (`1 lb` / `1/2 medium` head) napa cabbage to `2 to 3 mm` pieces, toss with `5 g` (`1 tsp`) fine salt for `15 to 20 minutes`, transfer to a clean dish towel, and wring firmly until bone-dry (`~230 to 250 g`, discarding `~220 g` free water) `[s2, s5, s7]`.
- **Sequential Unidirectional Emulsion**:
  - **Layer 1 — Myosin Extraction (`2 minutes`)**: Beat `450 g` (`1 lb`) cold ground pork (`20% to 30%` fat, e.g., ground pork shoulder) with `5.5 g` (`1 tsp`, `1.22%` by meat weight) fine sea salt vigorously in **one single direction** with chopsticks or a stiff spatula for `2 full minutes` until pale and tacky `[s1, s5]`.
  - **Layer 2 — Hydro-Emulsion / `Da Shui` (`6 minutes`)**: Add the `75 mL` (`5 tbsp`) cold scallion-ginger water in `3` stages (`25 mL` / `~5 tsp` per stage), stirring continuously in the **same single direction** for `2 minutes` per stage until each addition disappears and the paste turns glossy, springy, and clings to the bowl (`da shang jin`) `[s1, s5]`.
  - **Layer 3 — Umami & Aromatics (`1 to 2 minutes`)**: Add `2 tbsp` (`30 g`) light soy sauce, `1 tbsp` (`15 g`) oyster sauce, `1 tbsp` (`15 g`) Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry / dry Marsala), `1 tbsp` (`15 g`) finely grated fresh ginger, `1 tsp` (`4 g`) granulated sugar, and `1/2 tsp` (`1.5 g`) ground white pepper (plus optional `2 cloves` minced garlic and `1` egg white); stir `1 to 2 minutes` in the same direction `[s1, s5]`.
  - **Layer 4 — Lipid Cap, Chill & Late Cabbage Fold-In**: Beat `1 tbsp` (`14 g`) toasted sesame oil and `1 tbsp` (`14 g`) neutral oil into the meat paste in the same direction to seal the emulsion; cover and refrigerate `15 to 30 minutes` `[s5]`. Right before wrapping, fold in the `230 to 250 g` squeezed napa cabbage and `4 stalks` (`40 g`) finely chopped scallions (`~919 g` gross / `~914 g` net after microwave test) `[s3, s5]`. Microwave `1 tsp` filling for `15 to 20 seconds` (`10 to 30 seconds` range) until steaming hot and opaque to verify seasoning `[s1]`.

## 3. Pleating, Pan-Frying (`Guotie`), Steaming (`Zhengjiao`) & Dipping Sauce

- **Pleating & Freezer Protocol**:
  - Spoon `1 generous tbsp` (`24 to 25 g` for `4-inch` wrappers enclosing all `~914 g` across `36` skins; or `15 to 20 g` if rolling smaller `3.5-inch` skins across `~45` wrappers) onto the thicker center of each wrapper (keep the outer rim grease-free), fold into a Two-Way Symmetric Pleat (`2 to 4` pleats per side toward a center pinch) or One-Way Crescent Pleat (`4 to 6` front pleats), and press the base flat `[s2, s7, s10]`.
  - Cook immediately or tray-freeze uncovered in a single layer on parchment for `1 to 2 hours` before bagging (`1 to 3 months`); cook straight from frozen without thawing `[s2, s7]`.
- **Primary Cook — Pan-Fried Potstickers (`Guotie` Sear-Steam-Crisp)**:
  - Heat `1 tbsp` (up to `2 tbsp`) high-smoke-point neutral oil per batch in a lidded `10- to 12-inch` nonstick or seasoned cast-iron skillet over **medium-high** heat; arrange `12` dumplings flat-side down and sear undisturbed `2 to 3 minutes` until bottoms are lightly golden `[s2]`.
  - Pour in `1/3 cup` (`80 mL`, range `60 to 120 mL`) water using the lid as a splatter shield, cover immediately, lower heat to **medium-low**, and steam `5 to 6 minutes` (`8 to 10 minutes` if frozen) until wrappers look puffed and translucent and pork centers reach `160°F to 165°F` (`71°C to 74°C`), meeting the `160°F` (`71.1°C`) USDA ground pork safety minimum `[s2, s3, s12]`.
  - Uncover over **medium-high** heat for `1 to 2 minutes` (`1 to 3 minutes` range) until all water evaporates and bottoms re-crisp to deep amber-brown; serve immediately crisp-side up or hold crisp-side up on a wire rack in a `200°F` oven `[s2]`.
  - *Optional Crispy Lace Skirt (`Binghua`)*: Substitute `1/3 cup` (`80 mL`) water whisked with `1 tsp` AP flour, `1 tsp` cornstarch, and `1 pinch` salt in a lidded `10-inch` nonstick skillet; steam covered `5 to 6 minutes` fresh (`8 to 10 minutes` frozen) until pork centers reach `160°F`, then evaporate uncovered `3 to 10 minutes` over medium-low heat until golden lace pulls from the pan edges and invert onto a plate `[s8, s12]`.
- **Alternate Cook — Bamboo Steamer (`Zhengjiao`)**:
  - Line a `10- to 12-inch` two-tier bamboo steamer with perforated parchment or whole outer napa cabbage leaves from the remaining half head (never oil raw bamboo), space dumplings `1/2 to 1 inch` (`1.5 to 2.5 cm`) apart over `2 inches` (`5 cm`) boiling water across `2 to 3` uncrowded batches (replenishing boiling water between batches), and steam covered `8 to 10 minutes` fresh (`15 to 20 minutes` frozen) to `160°F+` `[s3, s7, s11, s12]`.
- **Classic Northern Dipping Sauce**:
  - Whisk `2 tbsp` Chinkiang black vinegar (or rice vinegar), `1 1/2 tbsp` light soy sauce, and `1/4 tsp` toasted sesame oil (plus optional `1 to 2 tsp` chili crisp/oil) `[s3]`.
