## Key Formulation Parameters from Report
- **Master Wrapper Doughs (300 g AP Flour Base -> 32–36 Wrappers)**:
  - **Option A — Hybrid Half-Scalded Dough (`Bantangmian`, 58.3% Hydration)**: 300 g (~2 1/2 cups spooned) all-purpose flour (or 150 g AP + 150 g cake flour), 3 g (1/2 to 3/4 tsp) fine salt, 120 g (1/2 cup) boiling water (100°C / 212°F) poured into the center while stirring, followed immediately by 55 g (3 1/2 tbsp, plus 1–2 tsp as needed) cool/room-temp water around the perimeter `[2, 3, 4]`. Gather into a rough ball, rest covered 20–30 minutes, knead 3–5 minutes to "earlobe softness", divide into 4 portions (~119 g each), and rest covered 30–60 minutes `[3, 4]`. Yields 478 g total (36 wrappers @ ~13.2 g each, rolled to 9–11 cm / 3.5–4.5 in diameter with 2–3 mm center belly and 1–1.5 mm thin outer edges) `[2, 3, 4, 7]`.
  - **Option B — 100% Hot-Water Dough (`Tangmian`, 60.0% Hydration)**: 300 g AP flour + 3 g salt + 175–185 g (180 g target / 3/4 cup) boiling water; cool 2–5 minutes, knead 3–5 minutes, rest 30 minutes `[2, 4]`.
  - **Option C — 100% Cold-Water Dough (`Lengmian`, 50–52% Hydration)**: 300 g AP flour + 2–3 g salt + 150–156 g (2/3 cup) cool water for boiled *shuijiao* `[1, 2, 7]`.
  - **Storage Rule**: Roll and fill in small batches of 5–12 wrappers under a damp towel; if stacking in advance, dust with cornstarch, potato starch, or tapioca starch—never wheat flour `[3, 7]`.
- **Master 4-Layer Northern Pork & Napa Cabbage Filling (~800 g Filling, 36–45 Dumplings @ 15–22 g each)**:
  - **Aromatic Water Infusion**: Steep 2 bruised scallions, 15 g smashed ginger, and 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (optional) in 100 mL (scant 1/2 cup) hot water for 15 minutes until cool; strain 75 mL (5 tbsp) `[1, 49]`.
  - **Purged Napa Cabbage**: Reserve 6–8 whole outer leaves if lining a bamboo steamer `[18]`. Finely mince 450 g (1 lb / 1/2 medium head) napa cabbage to 2–3 mm pieces, toss with 5 g (1 tsp) salt for 15–20 minutes, and wring bone-dry in a clean kitchen towel to ~230–250 g (~220 g water discarded) `[2, 5, 16, 17]`.
  - **Layer 1 (Protein Base)**: Stir 450 g (1 lb) cold ground pork (20–30% fat, e.g., pork shoulder) with 5.5 g (1 tsp, 1.22% of meat weight) fine sea salt vigorously in one single direction for 2 full minutes until pale and tacky `[1, 5]`.
  - **Layer 2 (Hydro-Emulsion / `Da Shui`)**: Whip in 75 mL (5 tbsp) cooled scallion-ginger water in 3 stages (25 mL each), stirring continuously in the same single direction for 2 minutes per addition (6 minutes total) until glossy, springy, and clinging to the bowl (`da shang jin`) `[1, 5]`.
  - **Layer 3 (Umami & Aromatics)**: Add 2 tbsp (30 g) light soy sauce, 1 tbsp (15 g) oyster sauce, 1 tbsp (15 g) Shaoxing wine, 1 tbsp (15 g) grated fresh ginger, 1 tsp (4 g) sugar, and 1/2 tsp (1.5 g) white pepper (plus optional 2 cloves minced garlic and 1 egg white); stir 1–2 minutes in the same direction `[1, 5]`.
  - **Layer 4 (Lipid Cap & Late Fold-In)**: Beat in 1 tbsp (14 g) toasted sesame oil and 1 tbsp (14 g) neutral or scallion-ginger oil, cover and chill 15–30 minutes, then fold in the squeezed cabbage (~230–250 g) and 3–4 stalks (40 g) finely chopped scallions dead last right before wrapping `[3, 5]`. Microwave-test 1 tsp filling for 10–30 seconds `[1]`.
- **Folding & Thermal Execution**:
  - **Folds**: Two-Way Symmetric Pleat (2–4 pleats per side toward center pinch) or One-Way Crescent Pleat (5–6 front pleats against smooth back) with ~1 tbsp filling `[2, 18, 53]`.
  - **Pan-Fried Potstickers (`Guotie` Sear-Steam-Crisp)**: Sear 10–15 dumplings in 1–2 tbsp neutral oil in a 10-to-12-inch nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for 2–3 minutes until golden; add 60–120 mL (1/4–1/2 cup; 80 mL / 1/3 cup optimal) water, cover tightly, and steam over medium-low heat 5–6 minutes fresh (7–12 minutes frozen) until skins are translucent and center hits 71–74°C (160–165°F); uncover and cook 1–3 minutes until water evaporates and bottoms re-crisp deep amber `[2, 15, 20]`.
  - **Crispy Lace Skirt (`Binghua`) Option**: Replace plain steaming water in a 10-inch nonstick skillet with 60–80 mL (1/3 cup) water + 1 to 1 1/2 tsp AP flour + 1 to 1 1/3 tsp cornstarch + 1 pinch salt; steam covered 3–6 minutes, then evaporate uncovered 3–10 minutes over medium-low heat and invert onto a plate `[25, 41, 77]`.
  - **Bamboo Steamer (`Zhengjiao`) Option**: Line bamboo steamer with reserved napa cabbage leaves or perforated parchment (never oil raw bamboo), space 1.5–2.5 cm (1/2–1 inch) apart over 5 cm (2 inches) boiling water, and steam covered 8–10 minutes fresh (15–20 minutes frozen) to 160–165°F `[3, 17, 18, 60]`.
  - **Dipping Sauces**: Classic Northern Dip (2 tbsp Chinkiang black vinegar + 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce + 1/4 tsp toasted sesame oil) `[3]` or Ultimate Dipping Sauce (1 tsp sugar in 1 tbsp hot water + 1 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tsp rice vinegar + 1 tsp minced ginger + 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil + 1 minced scallion white + chile crisp to taste) `[17]`.
